Quick check

Which statement best explains why the metal is a catalyst?

Core teaching ideaThe metal is a reusable molecular workbench: reactants bind temporarily, bonds become easier to rearrange, products leave, and the active site becomes available again.
Model limits and important caveats
  • Real converters contain huge numbers of active sites on a porous washcoat; this drawing shows one small region.
  • Surface mechanisms are simplified and may involve several intermediates and neighboring sites.
  • A catalyst lowers the barrier rather than removing it; real converters must warm up before reaching high effectiveness.
  • “Less harmful” does not mean harmless. Catalytic converters do not eliminate CO₂, which remains a greenhouse gas.
  • This model represents a gasoline-engine three-way catalyst operating near the proper air–fuel ratio. Other engines can use different after-treatment systems.
